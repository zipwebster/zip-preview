The challenge ↓

How much talent
can we afford to waste?

Young people are being shut out of employment, education and training. The cost is theirs to bear - in lost earnings, confidence and independence. It is also a loss for the whole country.

We need to be more ambitious and creative in how we tackle the NEETs crisis. That means testing approaches that challenge accepted practice, creating opportunities where they are missing, and giving new ideas the backing to prove themselves.

What would you do differently?

100 innovative
ideas

We’re calling on young people as well as entrepreneurs, youth sector workers and funders to help find and develop 100 bold ideas for getting young people into employment, education or training.

Your idea could change how employers recruit or how young people learn. It could create jobs for people others overlook, or tackle a barrier you’ve seen first-hand.

You can share an idea of your own or nominate one you’ve seen work elsewhere. What would it take to try it in your area?

Who can share an idea?

We welcome ideas from young people, youth sector workers, entrepreneurs and funders.

What happens next?

We’ll review the ideas and bring people together to explore the most promising ones. We’re planning a workshop in Sheffield where young people, organisers and potential backers can work out how to put an idea to the test.

What makes a strong idea?

  • Try something different. What would you do that people haven’t tried, or haven’t tried in this way?
  • Show what it could change. How would it open up opportunities for young people?
  • Imagine a first test. What could you try on a small scale, and what would you need to get started?

Does my idea need to be ready to launch?

An early idea is welcome. If something is already working, tell us where, who it helps and how it could reach more young people. You can nominate someone else’s idea.

What does NEET mean?

NEET stands for “not in education, employment or training”. This campaign seeks innovative ideas to help young people into employment, education or training.

Is this a funding application?

This is a call for ideas. Our ambition is to find, develop and build backing for promising approaches. Submitting an idea does not guarantee funding or support; details of any future opportunities will be shared separately.

A national conversation on innovation for the NEETs challenge.

NEET.ideas is a CIVA-led campaign to find 100 bold ideas for tackling the NEETs crisis.

It starts from a simple belief: young people themselves, as well as people working in businesses, charities, education and communities have ideas that deserve a wider hearing. We want to bring those ideas together, help promising approaches develop, and build the support to put them into practice.

We’re also bringing entrepreneurs, innovators and funders into a more ambitious conversation about what could work - and what it would take to make it happen.
